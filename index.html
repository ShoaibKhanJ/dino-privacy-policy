Privacy Policy for Dino Rush

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Dino Rush ("we", "our", or "the extension") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Dino Rush is designed to minimize data collection whenever possible. Depending on the features you use, the extension may process:

  • Extension settings and preferences.
  • Focus session data and productivity statistics.
  • Tab management information required for extension functionality.
  • Website blocking preferences selected by the user.

We do not collect sensitive personal information unless explicitly stated.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to provide and improve the functionality of Dino Rush, including:

  • Managing focus sessions.
  • Tracking productivity progress.
  • Providing tab organization features.
  • Maintaining user preferences.
  • Improving extension performance and reliability.

3. Data Storage

Most extension settings and productivity data are stored locally in your browser using Chrome Extension storage mechanisms.

If cloud synchronization is introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information to third parties.

Information may only be disclosed when required by law or to comply with legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

Dino Rush may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

7. Children's Privacy

Dino Rush is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
