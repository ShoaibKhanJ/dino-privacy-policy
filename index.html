Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Dino Rush respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed by the extension, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. About Dino Rush

Dino Rush is a productivity-focused Chrome extension designed to help users stay focused, manage browser activity, build productive habits, and reduce digital distractions through a gamified experience.

2. Information We May Process

To provide core functionality, Dino Rush may process:

  • User preferences and extension settings.
  • Focus session information.
  • Tab management data required for extension features.
  • Website blocking configurations created by the user.
  • Productivity statistics generated by the extension.

Dino Rush is designed to minimize data collection whenever possible.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the extension is used solely for providing and improving extension functionality, including:

  • Running focus sessions.
  • Managing browser tabs.
  • Tracking productivity progress.
  • Maintaining user preferences.
  • Providing gamification and achievement systems.
  • Improving performance and reliability.

4. Data Storage

Most data generated by Dino Rush is stored locally within your browser using Chrome Extension storage technologies.

Dino Rush does not intentionally transmit personal information to external servers unless such functionality is clearly disclosed in future updates.

5. Data Sharing

Dino Rush does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information may only be disclosed if required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Third-Party Services

The extension or website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Dino Rush is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Dino Rush is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in functionality, legal requirements, or business practices.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
